# Past Simple: The History Book

> [!abstract] **The Concept in a Nutshell**
> Use this tense to talk about **completed actions** that happened at a **specific, finished time** in the past. It is the language of history, storytelling, and memories!

---

## Scene Study (Real-World Dialogue)
> [!example] **Monday Morning Chat**
> **Jane:** "How **was** your weekend, Dave?"
> **Dave:** "It **was** great! I **went** hiking on Saturday, **saw** a beautiful waterfall, and **slept** under the stars."
> **Jane:** "Wow! **Did you take** any photos?"
> **Dave:** "Yes, I **took** a lot, but I **didn't post** them yet."

---

## The Blueprint (Form & Structure)
How to build it without sounding like a robot:

`[Subject] + [Verb (Past Form - V2)]`

- **Positive (+):** *She **visited** Rome last year.* / *They **bought** a house.*
- **Negative (-):** *I **did not (didn't) work** yesterday.* (Verb goes back to base form!)
- **Question (?):** ***Did** you **call** me?* (Verb goes back to base form!)

> [!tip] **Regular vs. Irregular:**
> - Regular verbs: Add **-ed** (*walk $\rightarrow$ walked, plan $\rightarrow$ planned*).
> - Irregular verbs: Have unique forms that you must memorize (*go $\rightarrow$ went, see $\rightarrow$ saw, buy $\rightarrow$ bought*).

---

## Mnemonic & Memory Hacks
> [!tip] **The "Did" Vampire**
> Think of **DID** in negatives and questions as a vampire that **sucks the past tense** out of the main verb!
> - Positive: *I **went**.* (V2 is active).
> - Negative: *I didn't **go**.* (Did took the past tense; *went* turns back into *go*).
> - Question: *Did you **go**?* (Did took the past tense; *went* turns back into *go*).

---

## Interactive Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)
- What is the past form of the verb 'to be'? :: Was (for I/he/she/it) and Were (for you/we/they).
- When do we use Past Simple instead of Present Perfect? :: When we specify a finished past time (e.g., yesterday, in 2020, two days ago).
- Correct this: "I didn't liked the food." :: "I didn't like the food." (Did takes the past tense, so like must be base form).

---

## The Trap (Common Mistakes)
> [!danger] **Avoid the Blunder!**
> - **The Mistake:** *"Did you saw him yesterday?"*
> - **The Fix:** *"Did you **see** him yesterday?"*
> - **Why?:** *Did* is already past. Never use two past forms (*did* + *saw*) together in a question!
