# Past Continuous: The Interrupted Photo

> [!abstract] **The Concept in a Nutshell**
> Use this tense to show an **action in progress at a specific moment in the past**, or to describe the background of a story. It is frequently interrupted by a shorter action (in Past Simple).

---

## Scene Study (Real-World Dialogue)
> [!example] **A Crime Scene Witness Interview**
> **Detective:** "What **were you doing** at 10 PM last night?"
> **Witness:** "I **was watching** a crime documentary in bed."
> **Detective:** "And what happened next?"
> **Witness:** "While I **was watching** the show, I **heard** a loud scream outside. When I looked, a man **was running** down the street."

---

## The Blueprint (Form & Structure)
How to build it:

`[Subject] + [was / were] + [Verb + -ing]`

- **Positive (+):** *I **was sleeping** when the alarm went off.*
- **Negative (-):** *They **were not (weren't) paying** attention during class.*
- **Question (?):** ***Were** you **driving** fast when the police stopped you?*

---

## Mnemonic & Memory Hacks
> [!tip] **The "Photo vs. Video" Rule**
> - **Past Simple (The Photo):** A single completed action.
>   * *I ate dinner.* 📸 (Done).
> - **Past Continuous (The Video):** An ongoing process in the past.
>   * *I was eating dinner.* 🎥 (Action in progress over time).
>
> **When they meet:**
> The video (Past Continuous) is running, and suddenly a photo (Past Simple) flashes and interrupts it!
> - *I **was reading** a book 🎥 when my cat **jumped** on my head 📸.*

---

## Interactive Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)
- Which connector do we use with Past Continuous vs. Past Simple? :: We often use "while" or "as" with Past Continuous (ongoing action) and "when" with Past Simple (interrupting action).
- Correct this: "I was knowing the answer when the teacher asked." :: "I knew the answer when the teacher asked." (Know is a stative verb and cannot be continuous).
- What does it mean if two Past Continuous verbs are joined by "while"? :: It means they were happening at the same time (parallel actions, e.g., "While I was studying, my brother was playing drums").

---

## The Trap (Common Mistakes)
> [!danger] **Avoid the Blunder!**
> - **The Mistake:** *"I playing video games when my mom called."*
> - **The Fix:** *"I **was** playing video games when my mom called."*
> - **Why?:** Never omit the auxiliary verb *was/were*. *Verb-ing* cannot exist in past continuous without it!
