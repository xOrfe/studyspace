# Present Simple: The Habit Machine

> [!abstract] **The Concept in a Nutshell**
> Use this tense to talk about **automatic habits, daily routines, eternal truths**, and things that are generally true. It is the language of science, schedules, and consistency!

---

## Scene Study (Real-World Dialogue)
> [!example] **A Day in the Life of a Workaholic**
> **Aria:** "Do you ever sleep, Leo?"
> **Leo:** "Of course! I **wake up** at 5 AM, I **drink** three cups of black coffee, and I **work** until midnight."
> **Aria:** "That **doesn't sound** very healthy. **Does your doctor know** about this?"
> **Leo:** "He **does**, but he **works** the same hours!"

---

## The Blueprint (Form & Structure)
How to build it without sounding like a robot:

`[Subject] + [Verb (Base Form) / Verb+s (for He/She/It)]`

- **Positive (+):** *They **play** video games every weekend.* / *She **plays** video games every weekend.*
- **Negative (-):** *I **do not (don't) eat** meat.* / *He **does not (doesn't) eat** meat.*
- **Question (?):** ***Do** you **live** here?* / ***Does** she **live** here?*

> [!tip] **Third-Person Singular spelling rule**
> - Most verbs: Add **-s** (*run $\rightarrow$ runs*)
> - Ends in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o: Add **-es** (*watch $\rightarrow$ watches, go $\rightarrow$ goes*)
> - Ends in consonant + y: Change -y to **-ies** (*study $\rightarrow$ studies*)

## Mnemonic & Memory Hacks
> [!tip] **The "Always & Never" Signal**
> Present Simple loves frequency adverbs! Remember the **SONG** acronym for positioning:
> **S**ubject + **O**ften/Usually/Always + **N**ormal Verb
> **G**o and put it after **Am/Is/Are**!
> - *I **always study** English.* (Before normal verb).
> - *I **am always** happy.* (After to be).

## Interactive Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)
- When do we use Present Simple for the future? :: For fixed timetables (e.g., "The train leaves at 9 PM").
- What is the negative form of "He flies"? :: He does not fly.
- Correct this: "She don't like chocolate." :: "She doesn't like chocolate." (She is third-person singular).

---

## The Trap (Common Mistakes)
> [!danger] **Avoid the Blunder!**
> - **The Mistake:** *"Does he plays tennis?"*
> - **The Fix:** *"Does he **play** tennis?"*
> - **Why?:** *Does* already takes the "-s" penalty! The main verb must stay in its base form.
