# Dependent Prepositions: The Magnetic Collocations

> [!abstract] **The Concept in a Nutshell**
> Unlike prepositions of time and place, **dependent prepositions** have no spatial logic. They are grammatical magnets, permanently glued to specific verbs, adjectives, or nouns. You must learn them as inseparable word pairs!

---

## Scene Study (Real-World Dialogue)
> [!example] **The Project Pitch**
> **Max:** "Are you still interested **in** joining our team? It depends **on** your schedule."
> **Chloe:** "Yes! I'm really good **at** organizing tasks, and I'm excited **about** the project."
> **Max:** "Great. Leo apologized **for** his delay. He wants to help us find a solution **to** the software issue."
> **Chloe:** "Awesome, I look forward **to** collaborating with him!"

---

## The Blueprint (Form & Structure)

### 1. Adjective + Preposition
- **interested in:** *She is interested **in** machine learning.*
- **good / bad at:** *He is good **at** coding, but bad **at** designing.*
- **afraid of:** *They are afraid **of** failure.*
- **different from:** *The new version is different **from** the old one.*
- **famous for:** *This city is famous **for** its street food.*
- **proud of:** *I am so proud **of** your progress.*

### 2. Verb + Preposition
- **depend on:** *Our release date depends **on** the testing phase.* (Never *depend of*)
- **apologize for / to:** *He apologized **to** the team **for** the mistake.*
- **listen to:** *You need to listen **to** the user feedback.* (Never *listen feedback*)
- **belong to:** *This domain name belongs **to** us.*
- **complain about:** *Users are complaining **about** the slow load time.*
- **wait for:** *We are waiting **for** the build to finish.*

### 3. Noun + Preposition
- **reason for:** *What is the reason **for** this sudden crash?*
- **solution to:** *We finally found a solution **to** the bug.*
- **influence on:** *Social media has a huge influence **on** teenagers.*
- **relationship with:** *He has built a strong relationship **with** his clients.*

---

## Mnemonic & Memory Hacks
> [!tip] **The "Collocation Glue"**
> - Never study verbs or adjectives in isolation! When writing down new vocabulary, always write it with its prepositional partner:
>   * `depend ON 🔗`
>   * `good AT 🎯`
>   * `married TO 💍`
> - Treat them as single, hyphenated words in your mind: *depend-on*, *listen-to*, *succeed-in*.

---

## Interactive Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)
- What preposition follows the adjective "married"? :: to (e.g., "She is married to an engineer").
- What preposition follows "succeed"? :: in (e.g., "They succeeded in launching the app").
- Correct this: "It depends of the weather." :: "It depends on the weather." (Depend always takes 'on').

---

## The Trap (Common Mistakes)
> [!danger] **Avoid the Blunder!**
> - **The Mistake:** *"I am angry with your decision."*
> - **The Fix:** *"I am angry **about / at** your decision."*
> - **Why?:** Use **with** for people (*"I am angry with you"*) and **about / at** for situations, events, or decisions (*"I am angry about the fee"*).
